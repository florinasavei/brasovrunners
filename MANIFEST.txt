Current mode: M1 complete in code - event pages, the whole of an event in the backoffice
(create, duplicate, configure, publish, archive, delete), the full registration lifecycle,
capacity and the waiting list, staff sign-in, legal document versioning, transactional email and
a registrations backoffice all exist and are tested. QA is deployed and the participant journey
can be walked there: every environment but production carries clearly marked sample legal text.
What remains is account creation and DevOps, not application code: a Mailgun account, the club's
.ro domain, the production Neon and Vercel projects, and the club's approved privacy notice,
terms and declaration text. WEEKEND.md records the narrower pilot this replaced. Agents start at
CLAUDE.md.

Milestones, in the owner's order:
- M1 Launch: event pages + complete registration journey + minimal backoffice + live email
- M2 Race features: multi-distance races, bibs, results with consent, backoffice completeness
- M3 Announcements: event updates with approval, notices to registered participants
- M4 Runner profiles
- M5 Mini CMS: articles, pages, galleries

Important current decisions:
- Mobile-first: the phone is the design target (BR-BUS-041)
- Next.js modular monolith
- Material UI
- Romanian + English i18n
- Mini CMS with Tiptap OSS
- Staff-only Auth.js authentication with the Zitadel OAuth provider, allowlisted by the
  staff_users table (DECISIONS.md §26)
- Passwordless participant flows via verified email action links
- Case-insensitive canonical email duplicate prevention plus Gmail dot/+ normalization,
  with gmail.com and googlemail.com collapsed to one identity
- Privacy-notice acknowledgment recorded at registration submission
- Declaration acceptance required before confirmation
- Privacy notice, terms, and declaration are versioned legal documents; no editor in V1
- Public free-place count, waiting list, timed promotion offers
- Waiting-list entries close when the event starts
- Restart of a cancelled/expired registration skips re-verification when already verified
- State-aware email resend, including a status notice for cancelled/expired registrations
- Optional public runner profiles with Strava/social links, noindex and absent from sitemap
- Capacity correctness comes from transaction-time expiry, not from the scheduler
- qa -> QA, main -> production; no develop branch
- Local checks and CI are one command: .githooks/pre-commit and the CI workflow both run
  yarn check; hooks are dependency-free via core.hooksPath, not husky
- Hosting: Vercel Hobby, region fra1, one project per environment; app stays portable by rule
- Custom domain bound at the end of M1, before launch; SETUP.md section 26 is the only hostname table
- .ro domain at a ROTLD-accredited registrar once registered, transferable; Cloudflare R2 for media
- Structured data (SportsOrganization, SportsEvent, Article) required on public pages
- Public content must be server-rendered and state its facts in text, for search and AI assistants
- AI retrieval crawlers allowed; training-crawler policy is an owner decision
- Read-only Claude/Codex repository integration; no push/deploy/secrets
  (a local agent operated by a human developer on a branch is unaffected)

- Engineering priority order in AGENTS.md section 1.5: trust-carrying correctness first,
  then legibility to a stranger, since most changes will be made by an AI agent

Open owner decisions are listed in BUSINESS.md section 9. Baseline BR-V1.4 decisions are

Open proposals awaiting a decision: DECISIONS.md section 8 (race photo hosting) and
